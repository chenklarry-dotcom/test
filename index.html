Our story

Small batches, honest sourcing

Amber & Oak started in 2013 as a single roaster in a garage on Maple Street. We still roast in small batches, print tasting notes on every bag, and know most of our regulars by name.

  • Direct-trade relationshipsLong-term partnerships with six family farms across three countries.
  • Roasted in 5kg batchesSmall enough to taste the difference between harvests, every time.
  • Compostable packaging, alwaysBags, cups, and lids that break down — not just recycling symbols.
